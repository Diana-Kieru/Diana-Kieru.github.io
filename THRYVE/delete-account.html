Delete your THRYVE account

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This page explains how to delete an account for the THRYVE Android app (Google Play package com.thryve, developer: Watany Technologies). You can request deletion without reinstalling the app. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1 — In the app (recommended)

  1. Open THRYVE and sign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings (from the main navigation).
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm your identity (password or Google re-authentication) and confirm deletion.

This removes your Firebase login, cloud backup (Firestore and Storage where enabled), and local app data for that account on the device.

Option 2 — Web / email request (no app reinstall required)

If you cannot open the app, no longer have the device, or prefer not to use in-app deletion, email us from the address linked to your THRYVE account:

  • thryveeapp@gmail.com

Include:

  • Subject line: Account deletion request
  • The email address registered on your THRYVE account
  • A short statement that you want the account and associated data deleted

We will verify ownership and delete your account and associated personal data within 30 days of a verified request. You do not need to reinstall THRYVE to use this path.

What is deleted

  • Your login account (Firebase Authentication)
  • Journal entries, gratitude notes, fasting history, Future Me letters, tasks, challenges progress, and related cloud sync data under your user record
  • Journal media stored in cloud backup under your account (Firebase Storage, when enabled)
  • Local app data for that account on devices after deletion completes or you sign out / clear data

What may remain temporarily

  • Aggregated analytics that cannot identify you
  • Data required for legal, security, or fraud prevention (retained only as long as necessary)
  • Content in shared multiplayer challenges created by other users (your account/profile is removed; group data may retain anonymized participation history)
